## The ruling as recommended (assistant's words)

- A fresh Opus agent, started without this session's context, that authored none
  of the code, evidence or preparation under review, counts as an independent
  reviewer, as a fresh Fable agent already does.
- Known limit: the builders are Opus agents, so an Opus reviewer may share their
  blind spots. Accepted for evidence reviews that are mostly mechanical
  (checksums, byte identity, timing method, claims versus evidence).
- For reviews that turn on design judgment, such as a contract amendment, the
  coordinator should propose a fresh Fable reviewer again.
- Independence still cannot be authenticated by any checker; this is an owner
  ruling, not proof.
